A curated map of where crypto is earned, held, and traded.

Five categories, one page: faucets that pay out small amounts for simple tasks, ad networks built around crypto traffic, cloud mining platforms, wallets to hold what you earn, and exchanges to trade it. Each listing links straight through to the site.

⚠️ Do your own research first. Payouts, fees, and terms on faucet, ad-network, and cloud-mining sites change often and vary by region — confirm current terms, minimum withdrawals, and reviews directly on each site before relying on it. Never enter a wallet's private key or seed phrase into any faucet, ad, or mining site.
